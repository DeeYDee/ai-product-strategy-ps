## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):**
We are evolving network performance monitoring from detecting problems to autonomously recovering networks—and every recovery makes the system better at the next one.

**The case:**
1. Why now:
Network environments are becoming increasingly distributed and complex across cloud, WAN, SD-WAN, Wi-Fi, data centers and applications.
Traditional monitoring produces more alerts; AI now makes it possible to correlate those signals, diagnose failures and automate recovery.
The window is to establish the autonomous network operations layer before large observability and infrastructure platforms bundle the capability.

2. What's defensible:
Our moat is not the underlying AI model.
It is the compounding dataset and control system:
Network state > failure signature > diagnosis > remediation > outcome
Every successful and failed remediation improves the system.

3. The economics:
The model is designed around cost per device/month, not seats.
AI COGS is controlled through:
Deterministic filtering > low-cost triage > frontier reasoning only when required
As scale increases:
More networks > more outcomes > better automation > lower cost per recovery > higher margin
Target: 70%+ gross margin at scale, with AI COGS declining as a percentage of revenue as automation and routing improve.

**The risks:**
1. Trust / failure modes:
Autonomous network changes create real operational risk.
Mitigation: confidence thresholds, deterministic policy gates, blast-radius controls, HITL escalation, rollback, validation and continuous red-team testing.

2. Scale / governance:
More autonomy increases the need for traceability, reliability monitoring and regulatory controls.
Mitigation: full decision/action audit trails, automated evaluations, drift monitoring, governance reviews and explicit autonomy boundaries.

3. Competitive:
Cisco, SolarWinds and major observability platforms can bundle AI-driven diagnosis and remediation.
Mitigation: move faster toward remediation intelligence and autonomous recovery, rather than competing on generic monitoring or model capability.

**The ask:**
Demonstrate a measurable, safe and economically viable autonomous recovery loop that improves with every incident.
